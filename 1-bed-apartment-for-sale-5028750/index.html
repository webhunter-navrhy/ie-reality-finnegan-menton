Description

** RIVER VIEWS, ATTIC SPACE, HIGH CEILINGS, BALCONY, PARKING **

Finnegan Menton are delighted to offer No. 66 Bellevue to the market.

Boasting fantastic features not commonly associated with apartment living, this beautifully presented apartment benefits from almost 10-foot-high ceilings which create an incredible sense of space and light that ensure a bright and spacious feel throughout, which, when combined with its excellent views towards the river, create a wonderful sense of tranquillity and connection with the leafy riverside setting. In addition, No. 66 offers attic storage which other residents have converted (s.p.p.).

Over the years, it has been tastefully and cleverly upgraded, with a modern fitted kitchen with glazed splashback, upgraded sink and induction hob, ample storage, and EcoVolt radiators with thermostats for improved energy efficiency. A balcony that offers excellent views - an ideal spot to enjoy morning coffee, unwind in the evening or simply appreciate the ever-changing riverside outlook - and secure designated parking, are among its many notable features.

Bellevue is a much sought after, well managed and impeccably maintained riverside development, located beside the War Memorial Gardens, and No. 66 is enviably located in Block E, on the top floor of that section of the building, accessible via lift, and ideally positioned to the rear of the development making it river, gardens, and water feature adjacent.

The development enjoys the best of both worlds offering a quiet and picturesque setting with large open green spaces, tucked away from the busy City, while still enjoying every conceivable amenity within easy reach and excellent connectivity via multiple 24 hour bus routes nearby. Heuston South Quarter, Phoenix Park, Kilmainham Gaol, IMMA, Royal Hospital Kilmainham, and the Hilton Hotel and Leisure Centre are all within walking distance. Islandbridge, and the nearby villages of Chapelizod and Kilmainham, offer many excellent cafes, eateries, and bars, and all the amenities that the City Centre have to offer are a short walk, cycle or bus ride away. Heuston station and the Luas are only 1.5km away, and the M50 and major road networks are easily accessible.
